About ZXForgen

ZXForgen is a gaming community built around the things we enjoy most — gaming, creating content, and meeting new people.

We started with a simple idea: create a place where gamers can enjoy their favourite games, share their content, and connect with others who have the same interests.

Our main focus is Minecraft, PvP, gaming content, and community projects. Whether you're here to play, watch, create, or just hang out, there's always something happening around ZXForgen.

We're not trying to make things complicated. It's just a community where people can come together, enjoy gaming, and build something of their own.

ZXForgen is still growing, and the journey has only started. New content, projects, and community features will continue to be added as we move forward.

Thanks for being a part of ZXForgen.
